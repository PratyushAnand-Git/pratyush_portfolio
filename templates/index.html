Engineering Background

About Pratyush Anand

I am a software engineer focused on building clean, high-concurrency systems and intuitive interfaces. My work spans from low-level architectural problem-solving to native Apple platform experiences and distributed civic intelligence platforms.

With projects ranging from RefineSimmer (an interactive simulation project crafted for Apple WWDC-26) to GumpWiser (civic AI rumor detection for World Wide Vibes) and NomadID (decentralized mobile identity), I thrive in environments that require both systems rigor and UX precision.

I believe great software engineering isn't just about passing tests — it's about building resilient, well-documented architectures that respect user privacy, run performantly on constrained devices, and delight humans.
